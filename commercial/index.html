Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Norwich HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched quickly, throughout Norwich and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. After that, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to prevent a repeat, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Norwich HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Fewer outages, longer equipment life, lower energy bills: those are the results our commercial clients consistently report after implementing a program.
